About

Tilburg, NL

I’m a software developer and AI Lead based in Tilburg, specialising in AI, cybersecurity and software for healthcare.

On the side — CS2Locker

I’m the founder and lead developer of CS2Locker, an internationally used production platform with live market data, custom infrastructure and thousands of daily visitors.
